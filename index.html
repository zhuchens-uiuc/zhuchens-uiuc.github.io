Grounded Interactive Agents

I am a PhD candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Klara Nahrstedt.

My research focuses on grounded, interactive AI agents. I study how agents connect instructions and goals to available evidence, seek missing information through interaction, and use that information to answer questions and take actions. My recent work spans streaming video understanding and video-grounded web agents. I am also exploring grounded collaboration in embodied multi-agent systems.

Video-grounded web agentsIn preparation

Tab Zero

A unified browser interface that lets web agents inspect egocentric video and complete web tasks using shared browser actions.

Up to 8.0 percentage points higher zero-shot task success rate than uniform sampling on the filtered Ego2Web subset.

Streaming video understandingUnder review

StreamClarify

A training-free framework for frozen vision-language models, combining entropy-based clarification, temporal-pyramid KV caching, and semantic retrieval.

Built StreamClarify-QA: 1,837 QA pairs from 355 videos. Evaluated three VLM backbones, with macro accuracy gains of up to 8.2 percentage points over existing streaming video QA methods.

Weakly supervised visual learningNeurIPS, 2021

TransMIL

Transformer-based multiple instance learning that models correlations among image patches for whole-slide image classification.

Earlier work on learning from weak supervision, incorporating spatial context into visual representations.
